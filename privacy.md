# Neon Arc privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Neon Arc is an idle tower defense game. This page explains what the game does with your information.

## On your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, settings and run history) is saved on your device. It is not sent anywhere unless you turn on cloud save.

## Cloud save with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the game asks for two things: your email address, which the game shows so you know which account is signed in, and Google's "drive.file" permission, which only lets the game see and change the files it creates itself.

The game creates one folder called Neon Arc in your Google Drive, with one file in it that holds your game progress. It cannot see, change or delete any of your other files. Your sign-in is stored only on your device.

## What Neon Arc does not do

Neon Arc has no ads, analytics or tracking. It does not sell or share your information with anyone. The only service it talks to is Google, for sign-in and for your cloud save.

Neon Arc's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

To stop using cloud save, choose Sign out in the game's Settings, under Cloud save. To remove Neon Arc's access entirely, open your Google Account, go to Security, then Your connections to third-party apps and services, and remove Neon Arc. To delete the saved game itself, delete the Neon Arc folder in your Google Drive.
