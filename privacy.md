# Neon Arc privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Neon Arc is an idle tower defense game. This page explains what the game does with your information.

## On your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, settings and run history) is saved on your device.

## Your account and cloud save

The first time the game goes online, it makes a guest account for you, known by a random Player ID, and saves your progress to the cloud under that account so it isn't lost. If you choose to create an account, the game also keeps the email address and password you pick, so you can log in on another device.

Accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your password securely; the game itself does not keep it. Your save, with the name of the device it came from, is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, and only your own account can read or change it.

## What Neon Arc does not do

Neon Arc has no ads, analytics or tracking. It does not sell or share your information with anyone. The only service it talks to is Google Firebase, for your account and your cloud save.

## Removing your data

To have your account and its cloud save deleted, open an issue on the game's GitHub page and give your Player ID (shown under Settings, Account). You don't need to post your email address.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be opened as an issue on the game's GitHub page: https://github.com/zyrrix/neon-arc/issues

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
