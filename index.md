# Neon Arc

Neon Arc is an idle tower defense game with neon visuals, for Windows and Android. Hold the line against wave after wave of enemies, buy upgrades, unlock tiers and relics, and collect cards and modules as your tower grows.

## Cloud save

Signing in with Google is optional. It backs up your progress to a private folder in your own Google Drive, so you can pick up where you left off on another phone or PC.
